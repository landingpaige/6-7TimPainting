What Older Homes Need Before Repainting

A home with decades of paint history usually needs more prep time than a newer build, not more paint. Loose or peeling layers have to come off before new paint goes on, bare wood needs priming, and plaster walls sometimes need patching before they'll take a coat evenly. Skipping straight to color is tempting because it's the visible part of the job, but on a lot of Grosse Pointe's older homes, the prep work is what actually determines how long the new paint holds up.
