FAQ

Common Questions About
Cabinet Painting.

Is painting kitchen cabinets worth it compared to replacing them?

If your cabinet boxes and doors are solid and you like the layout, painting is usually a much smaller investment than new cabinets or refacing, and it changes the look of the whole kitchen. If the boxes are damaged or you want a new layout, replacement may make more sense. Tim will tell you honestly which applies during the walkthrough.

What cabinets can be painted?

Most solid wood and MDF cabinets paint well with the right prep and primer. Thermofoil and some laminate finishes are harder to paint reliably, so we look at your cabinets in person before recommending anything.

Do you paint bathroom vanities and built-ins too?

Yes. The same process works for bathroom vanities, built-in shelving, bookcases, mudroom lockers, and other millwork.

How long will my kitchen be out of service?

It depends on the number of doors and drawers, the finish, and drying time. We give you a schedule with the written estimate and keep the kitchen as usable as possible while the work is underway.

Do you paint cabinets in Rochester Hills and nearby communities?

Yes. We paint kitchen cabinets and built-ins throughout Rochester Hills, Troy, Birmingham, Lake Orion, Shelby Township, and surrounding Oakland and Macomb County communities.
