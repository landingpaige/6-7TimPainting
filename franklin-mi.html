Exterior Painting Considerations in Southeast Michigan

Southeast Michigan's freeze-thaw winters and humid summers are hard on exterior paint regardless of neighborhood — moisture gets into hairline cracks, freezes, and expands them further, while UV exposure fades and chalks lower-quality coatings over a few seasons. Spring and fall tend to offer the most reliable temperature and humidity windows for exterior work, though we plan around each property's specific exposure — shade from mature trees, as is common on wooded lots like many in Franklin, changes how long surfaces take to dry between coats.
