FAQ

Common Questions About
Commercial Painting.

Do you paint commercial buildings while they are in use?

Yes. We specialize in working around active businesses. We offer after-hours and weekend scheduling so your staff and clients aren't disrupted. For larger spaces, we can work section by section in a phased approach.

What types of commercial properties do you paint in Oakland Township and Metro Detroit?

We paint office suites, retail spaces, showrooms, medical and dental offices, lobbies, hallways, conference rooms, and professional buildings throughout Oakland Township, Lake Orion, Shelby Township, Rochester Hills, Metamora, and surrounding Metro Detroit communities.

Are you licensed and insured for commercial painting work?

Yes. Tim MacDonough Painting Company is fully licensed and insured. A certificate of insurance (COI) is available upon request for property managers and building owners.

Do you use subcontractors on commercial jobs?

Never. Every project is completed by Tim and his direct crew — no subcontractors, ever. You deal with the same team from the initial estimate through the final walkthrough.

How do you minimize disruption to my business during painting?

We plan every project around your schedule — nights, weekends, or phased room-by-room work. Full protective measures are in place before every session, and every space is cleaned and ready before business hours resume.
