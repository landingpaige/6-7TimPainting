Do you handle large or estate-size homes in Oakland Township and surrounding areas?

Yes. We regularly work on custom builds, historic estates, and large-scale renovation projects throughout Oakland Township, Lake Orion, Shelby Township, Rochester Hills, and Metamora. Complex architecture and detailed millwork are where we do our best work.

Are you the owner who actually shows up on-site every day?

Yes. Tim MacDonough is on-site every day of every project — not just at the estimate and final walkthrough. You'll never come home to a crew you haven't met or a job being managed by phone.
