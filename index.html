How It Works

A Process Built on
Preparation, Not Speed.

Most painting problems trace back to rushed prep work. Here's how we do it differently — every time, on every project.

1

Free Consultation & Written Estimate

We come to you — at no charge — walk through every area, answer every question, and hand you a written estimate with a clear scope and a guaranteed price. No vague language, no hidden line items.

2

Prep & Full Property Protection

Before a single can opens: floors covered, furniture moved and wrapped, hardware masked, trim taped, landscaping protected (exterior). The quality of your finish depends entirely on the quality of the prep — so we never rush it.

3

Expert Painting, Owner On-Site

Tim is on-site every day — not just available by phone. Precise application, proper drying time between coats, daily cleanup before we leave. Your home is treated with the same care we'd want for our own.

4

Final Walkthrough & Your Approval

We walk through together before we consider the job done. If anything isn't exactly right — edge, coverage, finish — we fix it. We don't pack up until you're satisfied. That's a promise, not a policy.
