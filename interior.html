FAQ

Common Questions About
Interior Painting.

How do you protect furniture and floors during interior painting?

Before any paint opens, furniture is moved and covered, floors are laid with drop cloth, and hardware is masked off. We treat your home with the same care we'd want for our own. Nothing gets paint on it by accident.

Do you repair walls before painting?

Yes — always. Holes, cracks, dings, and texture issues are patched, sanded, and primed before any color goes on. A smooth, properly prepared surface is what separates a lasting result from one that looks tired in a year.

What interior spaces do you paint in Oakland Township and nearby communities?

We paint walls, ceilings, trim, baseboards, crown molding, doors, cabinets, and built-ins in homes throughout Oakland Township, Lake Orion, Shelby Township, Rochester Hills, Metamora, and surrounding Metro Detroit communities.

Do you paint kitchen cabinets?

Yes. We repaint kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities, and built-in shelving to look factory-fresh — a high-value update compared to full cabinet replacement. Proper prep and the right primers make all the difference.

Can you complete a single room, or do you only take on whole-home projects?

Both. Whether it's one accent wall, a powder room refresh, or a full home repaint before a move-in, every job gets the same level of attention and prep. There's no job too small for the same standard of work.
